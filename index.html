Epigenetics & Genomics Researcher

I lead the Epigenetics program in Indigenous Genomics at The Kids Research Institute Australia, and hold a Senior Lecturer position at the Australian National University.

My research uses genomics technologies and advanced data analytics to improve chronic disease prediction, prevention, and treatment — with a focus on precision medicine for Indigenous Australians.
