Sam Buckberry, PhD

Epigenetics, Genomics and Computational Biology

I'm an NHMRC Emerging Leader Investigator Fellow, and lead the Epigenetics of Health and Disease program in Black Ochre Data Labs at The Kids Research Institute Australia. I'm also an Adjunct Senior Lecturer at the University of Western Australia, and serve on the WA Department of Health Human Research Ethics Committee.

We use genomics technologies and computational methods to predict, prevent, and treat disease. Our current work includes developing new knowledge of cardiometabolic disease and precision medicine tools with the PROPHECY cohort, combining population-scale epigenomics, genetics, and precision medicine to benefit Indigenous Australians.
